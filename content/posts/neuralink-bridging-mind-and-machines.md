<p class="has-small-font-size">Bridging minds & machines!! Well, you heard me right. Seems like a 90's retro movie showcasing the future 2000's where a chip is implanted in your brain and you interact with technology just by your thoughts. Sounds very unreal right ? Well, but that's the visionary Elon Musk owned Neuralink Corp. aims at. </p>



<p class="has-small-font-size">  The company has developed a brain-machine interface called "the Link" which is a surgically embedded neural-chip implant designed to decode and stimulate brain activity. </p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-base-color has-contrast-background-color has-text-color has-background has-link-color has-large-font-size"><p><span class="uppercase">Modus Operandi</span></p></h2>



<p class="has-small-font-size">To understand how the Neuralink works, we would have to dwell around the anatomy the humane CPU- The Brain. So let us try to understand how the brain works, before we learn how Neuralink has achieved this scientific marvel that was once considered a pseudoscience.</p>



<p class="has-small-font-size">The brain - a fascinating organ in the human body is comprised of 100 of billions of neurons. Each Neuron has a dendritic arbor (structured like a tree with branches), cell body and axon. These neurons together form a network through axon dendrite junction- called as the synapses. These billions of neurons communicate with each other via. signals called as the Neurotransmitters. Axon releases a neurotransmitter as a response to electric spike called as <em>Action Potential</em>. So now-what is an action potential? Well, it can be any cognitive function we perform-be it perception, feeling, hearing, thinking etc. So, when a neuron receives enough right amount of neurotransmitters as input it instantaneously triggers a chain reaction. This chain reaction triggers an internal relay as a message which is taken downstream synapses. This action potential produces an electric field that spreads from neuron that can be detected by placing electrodes thereby allowing the recording of information presented by a neuron. A continuous sequence of the function of brain and synapses have been provided in the below image.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img src="https://thequantumvoyager.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/brain-process-1.jpg?w=707" alt="" class="wp-image-214" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Courtesy: The Quantum Voyager</figcaption></figure>



<p class="has-small-font-size">Now that we have understood how the brain process information and how synapses come into play, let us inverse the final stage. Instead of detecting the electric impulse using a electrode, let the electrode create an electrical field that will in-turn trigger neuron, thus completing a feedback loop. This exact mechanism is salvaged by Neuralink for its mode of operation. </p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-text-align-left has-base-color has-contrast-background-color has-text-color has-background has-link-color has-large-font-size">Operational mechanics: <strong>Unlocking the Brain’s Potential</strong></h2>



<p class="has-small-font-size">Neuralink’s operation is like a symphony of science and engineering. Combining tiny, flexible threads, a surgical robot, and advanced microelectronics to create a brain-machine interface capable of reading and interpreting neural signals. So, lets see how it all comes together:</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-medium-font-size"><strong>1. Tiny, Flexible "Threads" - The Listeners of the Brain</strong></h2>



<p class="has-small-font-size">Imagine hair-thin, flexible threads equipped with sensors that can listen to your brain’s electrical signals. These threads, made of biocompatible materials (i.e. materials that won’t harm the brain), are so thin (5–50 micrometers wide) that they barely disturb the brain tissue.</p>



<p style="font-size:1.3rem"><em><span style="text-decoration: underline">Why Are They Special?</span></em></p>



<p class="has-small-font-size">Each thread has 32 electrodes that capture the electrical impulses of neurons. Up to 96 threads can be used at once, creating a network of 3072 electrodes—an incredible leap in the ability to understand brain activity.</p>



<p style="font-size:1.3rem"><em><span style="text-decoration: underline">Surface Treatment:</span></em></p>



<p class="has-small-font-size">The electrodes are treated with advanced materials like Iridium Oxide (IrOx) and PEDOT:PSS [<em>Poly(3,4-ethylenedioxythiophene): polystyrene sulfonate</em>], making them more effective at capturing signals without interfering with the brain's natural processes.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-medium-font-size"><strong>2. The Robot Surgeon - Precision at its Finest</strong></h3>



<p class="has-small-font-size">The placement of these threads is no small feat, and that’s where Neuralink’s neurosurgical robot shines. This machine is precise to the micrometer and ensures the threads are inserted exactly where they need to be.</p>



<p style="font-size:1.3rem"><em><span style="text-decoration: underline">How Does It Work?</span></em></p>



<p class="has-small-font-size">A tungsten needle threads each wire into the brain without damaging blood vessels. An imaging system guides the robot, tracking brain structures in real-time to avoid obstacles. It’s fast too—placing up to six threads (192 electrodes) per minute.</p>



<p style="font-size:1.3rem"><em><span style="text-decoration: underline">Why Use a Robot?</span></em></p>



<p class="has-small-font-size">Robots can perform this delicate task far more accurately than human hands, ensuring the brain remains unharmed during the process.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://thequantumvoyager.home.blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-edited.png" alt="" class="wp-image-252" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Source: The Quantum Voyager</figcaption></figure>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<h4 class="wp-block-heading has-medium-font-size">3. <strong>The Brain’s "Translator" - Custom Electronics</strong></h4>



<p class="has-small-font-size">Once the threads are in place, the signals they capture need to be processed and understood. Neuralink’s custom microchips act as the brain’s translator.</p>



<p style="font-size:1.3rem"><em><span style="text-decoration: underline">How It Works:</span></em></p>



<p class="has-small-font-size">These chips amplify, digitize, and process the signals captured by the electrodes. Each chip is compact yet powerful, processing signals from 256 channels simultaneously. All this data is then sent to an external device via a high-speed USB-C connection for real-time analysis.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />



<h4 class="wp-block-heading has-medium-font-size"><strong>4. Real-Time Communication with the Brain</strong></h4>



<p class="has-small-font-size">The final step involves interpreting the brain's signals and using them to control external devices like prosthetic limbs, computers, or even speech synthesizers. This process relies on advanced software that can detect brain activity (spikes) in real-time with high accuracy.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-base-color has-contrast-background-color has-text-color has-background has-link-color has-large-font-size"><strong> The Current and Future Potential</strong> of Neuralink</h3>



<p class="has-small-font-size">Imagine controlling your smartphone, typing messages, or navigating the web—without moving a muscle. Neuralink is not just a gadget from a sci-fi movie; it’s a glimpse into what might be our future. So let's take a look at the real time application and the potential of this device. </p>



<p class="has-secondary-color has-text-color has-link-color has-medium-font-size"><strong>Transforming Lives with Medical Breakthroughs</strong></p>



<p class="has-small-font-size">One of Neuralink’s most immediate goals is to revolutionize healthcare, especially for people with severe disabilities.</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li class="has-small-font-size"><strong>Restoring Mobility:</strong> Neuralink aims to help individuals with spinal cord injuries regain control of devices like wheelchairs or robotic limbs. Imagine a world where someone paralyzed could type on a screen or play a video game, all through thought.</li>



<li class="has-small-font-size"><strong>Treating Neurological Disorders:</strong> Conditions like epilepsy, Parkinson’s disease, and depression might be managed better by modulating brain signals. Neuralink could identify irregular brain activity and even offer corrective stimulation.</li>
</ul>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading has-secondary-color has-text-color has-link-color has-medium-font-size"><strong> A New Era of Communication</strong></h4>



<p class="has-small-font-size">For those who have lost the ability to speak due to conditions like ALS or stroke, Neuralink could be life-changing. By decoding brain activity associated with speech, it may enable users to communicate via text or synthesized speech. No voice? No problem. The brain does the talking.</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">

<li class="has-small-font-size"><strong>Knowledge Uploads:</strong> Remember the “I know kung fu” scene from <em>The Matrix</em>? Neuralink could someday enable us to acquire skills or languages by directly uploading data to the brain.</li>
